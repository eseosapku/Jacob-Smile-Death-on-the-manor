# Jacob Smile: Death on the Manor

## Game Concept

A first-person murder mystery detective game where you investigate a poisoning case. Your client, Primrose, is accused of murdering Hendrick Black. You have 5 puzzles to solve to prove her innocence and find the real killer—the butler.

---

## The 5 Puzzles

1. **UV Fingerprint Reveal** - Use UV light on the glass to reveal fingerprints
2. **Dice Game (SMILES)** - Select suspects and roll dice. Correct suspect = 6
3. **Word Arrangement** - Arrange 18 words: "The Poison is inside the Ice not in the milk..."
4. **Letter Collection** - Collect scattered letters to spell JOSEPH (the butler's name)
5. **Truth or Lie Quiz** - Answer 9 true/false questions about the butler's motive

---

## Controls

- **WASD** - Move around
- **Mouse** - Look around / Control UV stick and dice
- **Left Click** - Interact / Select words / Click buttons
- **Right Click** - Return last letter
- **Backspace** - Remove last word
- **ESC** - Pause

## How to Play

1. Read the on-screen instructions for each puzzle
2. Solve all 5 puzzles in order
3. Uncover the truth: the butler is the killer
4. Save Primrose from wrongful conviction
